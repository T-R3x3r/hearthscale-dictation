# Dictation

Hold a key and speak. Clean text appears in the program in front. The models run on your computer, so what you say stays with you.

Free, with no account.

## Get started

There is no window to open. Download the models the introduction offers, then hold **Ctrl+Shift+Space** in any program and speak. Let go, and the text appears where you were typing, in any language your model hears.

- **Live words** show as you speak, with a model that streams. With any other model, the whole text appears when you let go.
- **Clean up** removes filler words and false starts, keeps your last correction and fixes the punctuation. Choose a chat model to switch it on.
- **History** keeps each dictation on this computer, so you can copy it again. Open **Dictation: History** from the tray icon. Turn it off in Settings, and the history is deleted.

## Keys

Change the keys in **Settings > Apps > Dictation**. Choose a combination, or one key alone on Windows, such as Right Ctrl, Right Alt or Caps Lock.

Under **Key behaviour**:

- **Hold or tap** ends the dictation when you let go, or at the next press after a quick tap.
- **Hold** ends the dictation when you let go.
- **Tap** starts and ends the dictation with a press each.

Press **Esc** while you speak to drop the dictation. Press it during the clean up to skip it, and the words go in as they were heard. At any other moment Esc works as usual.

With one key alone, press another key with it to cancel the dictation. Ctrl+C still copies.

## Models

- **A speech model**, to hear you. Required. Under **Models** in **Settings > Apps > Dictation**, choose your languages, then a model.
- **A chat model**, to clean up the text. Optional. Gemma 4 E2B handles many languages.

## Recognition

- **Translate to English** writes English from any language, on a speech model that translates.
- **Filter silence** skips the parts with no voice before the speech model hears them.
- **Remove filler words** takes out sounds such as "um" and "uh", with no chat model.
- **Dictionary** holds names and terms you say. Write `Parakeet = para keet` to fix what the model keeps writing.

## Clean up

- **Match the style to the program** writes for a chat, an email or a coding tool.
- **Read the text around the cursor** sends up to 1,024 characters on each side of the cursor to your chat model, to settle what you meant. A password field is never read.
- **Correction word** is the word you say before a correction, such as "sorry".
- **Instructions** can be your own in place of the built-in ones.

## When something fails

If the speech model fails, History offers **Try again**, which hears the same recording again. **Test** beside the microphone in Settings records ten seconds and plays them back, and the sound stays on this computer.

## Permissions

- **Your microphone**, only while you dictate. Choose one in **Settings > Apps > Dictation**, or use the system's default.
- **To type into your other programs**, to insert the text.
- **To show a view over your other programs**, while you speak.
- **The name of the program in front**, to match the style of the text, and **the text around the cursor**. Both go to your chat model and nowhere else.
- **To keep running in the background**, so the key works at any moment.

The sound is not kept.
